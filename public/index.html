about me:

I'm a UX Designer with 5+ years of experience in B2B Healthcare SaaS, specializing in designing complex, data-heavy products at scale. My work sits at the intersection of design systems, enterprise dashboards, and AI-powered experiences, helping teams turn complexity into clarity.

I've led the design of high-scale platforms used across multiple business units—building and maintaining design systems that support distinct visual identities while ensuring consistency, speed, and scalability. I enjoy solving problems where large datasets, advanced filters, and operational workflows meet real business outcomes.

My experience includes designing AI-driven chatbot interfaces, no-code internal tools, bi-directional analytics dashboards, and enterprise-grade admin panels serving hundreds of users. I've also worked on some of the company's most impactful products, driving measurable improvements in adoption, productivity, and decision-making efficiency.
